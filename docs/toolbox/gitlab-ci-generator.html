GitLab CI generator 

Starter .gitlab-ci.yml: stages, caching, rules and registry jobs.

.gitlab-ci.yml 





    
 about this tool

    
Builds a .gitlab-ci.yml skeleton with stages, per-job rules restricted to your trigger branches, dependency caching and (for Docker) the standard CI_REGISTRY build-push job using the runner’s service account.
Add the deploy stage for a manual gated job — when: manual keeps promotions deliberate.
